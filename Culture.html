CULTURE

Yantai has a rich culture. Here we are showing some of them.

Haiyang Yangko

History: With a history of more than 600 years, it originated in the Ming Dynasty.

Feature: A lively folk dance with bold movements, bright costumes and strong rhythm.

Culture: Performed at festivals, celebrations and temple fairs. It shows joy, harvest and good wishes.

Level: National-level intangible cultural heritage of China, a treasure of Jiaodong culture.

Introduction: Haiyang Yangge has a long history, nearly 600 years ago, starting in the Ming Dynasty and prevailing in the Qing Dynasty. Initially, it was a folk sacrifice, blessing, and harvest celebration, and later gradually developed into a traditional performance of the Spring Festival, temple fairs, and major festivals.

Jiaodong Flower Steamed Buns

Highlight: Hand-sculpted steamed wheat buns; natural vegetable/fruit juice coloring (spinach, pumpkin, red yeast rice); soft, fragrant, edible art

Culture: Originated in Han Dynasty, flourished in Ming-Qing; listed as National Intangible Cultural Heritage. Used for Spring Festival (date buns, sacred worm), weddings (dragon-phoenix), birthdays (peach-shaped longevity buns), baby’s 100-day celebration

Tip: Eat fresh while warm; decorative pieces can be air-dried as souvenirs; no artificial colors, kid-friendly

Introduction: Yantai Flower Dumplings (Jiaodong Flower Dumplings) is an iconic folk pasta in Yantai and the entire Jiaodong region, with origins dating back to the Han Dynasty, and the Ming and Qing dynasties were finalized and prevalent; It is now a national intangible cultural heritage. Yantai flower dumplings have a history of more than 300 years, originating from Jiaodong folk customs, first used for sacrifices and blessings, and later gradually became an indispensable traditional pasta for important occasions such as festivals, weddings, and birthday celebrations.
