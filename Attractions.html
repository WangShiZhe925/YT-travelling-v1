Penglai Pavilion

Level:AAAAA Scenic Area, a National Key Cultural Relic Protection Unit.

Location:Northernmost of Jiaodong Peninsula, Penglai District

Highlights:Known as "Fairyland on Earth", birthplace of the Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea legend; great spot to watch mirages. It is one of China's Four Great Ancient Towers.

Tip:Visit at sunrise for the best sea-and-sky view.

Introduction:Penglai Pavilion is located on the top of Danya Mountain, Penglai District, Yantai City, Shandong Province, and is one of the four famous buildings in China, a national 5A-level scenic spot, a national key cultural relics protection unit, and is known as a wonderland on earth. It is not a single pavilion, but a huge group of ancient buildings composed of six major buildings (Mituo Temple, Dragon King Palace, Tianhou Palace, Penglai Pavilion Main Building, Sanqing Hall, Lüzu Hall) and ancillary buildings. It is also the birthplace of the legend of watching the "mirage" and "the Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea".

Yantai Mountain

Level: Key Historical Site, Symbol of Yantai

Location: Zhifu District, the birthplace of Yantai City

Highlights: Features 17 former consulate buildings of Britain, USA, Germany, Japan, etc.; Yantai Hill Lighthouse offers a 360° panoramic view of the coastal city; Bing Xin Memorial Hall is here (the writer spent her childhood here).

Tip: Walk the coastal plank road to enjoy the waves hitting the reefs.

Introduction: Yantai Mountain is located at the northern end of Zhifu District, Yantai City, Shandong Province, and is the birthplace and symbol of Yantai, a national AAAA-level scenic spot, and a national key cultural relics protection unit. It is 42.5 meters above sea level and is surrounded by the sea on three sides, with a total area of 45 hectares; It was named after the establishment of Langyan Duntai (Beacon Tower) in the thirty-first year of Ming Hongwu (1398), and the name of Yantai City is also derived from this. With a history of more than 600 years, it has the most complete and dense modern consulate complex in Asia, and is a unique landscape that blends mountains, seas, cities and ports.

Changyu Wine Culture Museum

Level: AAAA Scenic Area

Location: Dama Road, Zhifu District

Highlights: Highlights: A "living fossil" of China's wine industry. Founded in 1892, it displays century-old wine cellars, antique wine-making equipment, and offers wine-tasting experiences.

Tip: Book a guided tour to learn about the history of Chinese wine export.

Introduction: Changyu Wine Culture Museum is located at No. 56 Amasa Road, Zhifu District, Yantai City, Shandong Province (in the courtyard of the former site of Changyu Company), and is the first world-class wine museum in China, a national AAAA-level tourist attraction, a national first-class museum, the first batch of national industrial tourism demonstration bases (top ten), and a patriotic education base of the China Federation of Overseas Chinese. It was opened in 1992 during the centennial celebration, expanded twice in 2002 and 2007, and completed the digital 4.0 upgrade in 2022 (Changyu's 130th anniversary). The museum area includes the wine culture square, the main exhibition hall, the century-old underground wine cellar, and the 1892 Art Center, which is an iconic carrier of Yantai's port opening culture and national industrial civilization.

Yangma Island

Level: AAAA Tourist Resort

Location: Muping District, northeast of Yantai

Highlights: Also called "Royal Horse Island", with a legend linking to Emperor Qin Shi Huang. It has golden beaches, crystal-clear seawater, and offers activities like sea fishing, beach camping, and coastal cycling.

Tip: Rent an electric bike to circle the entire island.

Introduction: Yangma Island (also known as Elephora Island), located in the Yellow Sea, 9 kilometers north of Muping District, Yantai City, Shandong Province, is a national AAAA-level tourist attraction, a provincial-level tourist resort and a provincial-level geopark. The total area is 13.52 square kilometers, and the coastline is about 19.5 kilometers long; Named after the legend of Qin Shi Huang raising imperial horses here during his eastern tour, there is a unique landform of "one island and three beaches" (black mud flats in front of the island, golden sand beaches on the east side of the island, and reef beaches behind the island), with a forest coverage rate of more than 60% and clear sea water, known as the "Oriental Hawaii" and "Northern Glass Sea Secret Land" Muping District Government.
