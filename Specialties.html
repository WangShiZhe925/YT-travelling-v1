SPECIALTIES

There are many kinds of delicious foods in Yantai. We will show some of them below.

Penglai Noodles

Highlight: Thin chewy noodles with red sea bream (pagrosomus major) seafood broth, a signature breakfast in Penglai

Culture: Over 200 years history, Yantai intangible cultural heritage; folk banquet staple in Penglai rural areas; famous as "Yi Futang Noodles"

Tip: Eat hot right after serving; pair with pickles for extra flavor

Introduction: Penglai small noodles are a traditional pasta dish in Penglai District, Yantai, Shandong Province, and a classic breakfast that Yantai people love. It is known for its fine noodles, fresh soup and marinated aroma, and is deeply integrated with the food culture of the seafood province.

Yantai Spanish Mackerel

Highlight: Large, thin-skinned, juicy; fresh mackerel meat leek filling, no fishy taste

Culture: Shandong intangible cultural heritage; legend linked to Qin Shi Huang’s east tour; Qi Jiguang rewarded soldiers with it in Ming Dynasty

Tip: Order at coastal restaurants in fishing season; serve with vinegar and ginger.

Introduction: Yantai mackerel dumplings are the iconic seafood pasta on the coast of Jiaodong, known for their size, thin skin, full filling, and fresh soup, which is the golden business card of Yantai's food culture and a must-have staple food for every family's festivals and hospitality.

Rice with Sea Worm

Highlight: Fresh local sea worm, crispy & tender; stir-fried with chives, rich umami sauce over rice.

History: A classic Yantai seafood dish; sea intestine used as "natural MSG" in Lu cuisine for hundreds of years.

Culture: Represents Jiaodong marine food culture; a must-try local signature meal.

Tip: Eat hot for the best taste.

Introduction: Sea sausage rice is a classic signature seafood dish in Yantai and Jiaodong, and it is also a representative umami delicacy of Shandong cuisine. With fresh sea sausage as the main ingredient, stir-fried with leeks, drizzled with rich fresh juice and covered with rice, the fresh aroma is strong, the taste is crisp and tender, and it is called "the most delicious seafood rice".

Yantai Menzi

Highlight: Sweet potato starch cake, pan-fried crispy outside & soft inside; topped with sesame paste, shrimp oil, garlic paste

Culture: Founded over 100 years ago, municipal intangible cultural heritage; iconic street food of Yantai

Tip: Eat at old downtown stalls; avoid over-saucing to keep original flavor

Introduction: Yantai stew is a very representative traditional street food in Yantai, Shandong, and a unique flavor delicacy in Jiaodong, made of sweet potato starch, fried until charred on the outside and tender on the inside, eaten with sesame sauce, shrimp oil, garlic paste, soft and glutinous, salty and fragrant, is a classic taste in the hearts of old Yantai people.

Yantai Apple

Highlight: Crisp, juicy, sweet; premium quality from the 37°N coastal climate

Culture: Introduced by American missionary John Nevius in 1871; China’s modern apple origin; national geographical indication product

Tip: Best season September–November; fresh apples keep well in cold storage

Introduction: Yantai apple is one of the most famous apple brands in China, a national geographical indication product, famous throughout the country for its large size, bright color, sweet flesh, juicy and crispy taste, known as "the first city of Chinese apples", and is the most representative specialty business card of Yantai.

Yantai Cherries

Highlight: Large, bright red, sweet and fragrant; known as "Northern Ruby"

Culture: Introduced in 1871 together with apples; Fushan district is the core producing area; national geographical indication product

Tip: Peak season May–June; eat fresh, avoid long storage

Introduction: Yantai cherry is a famous high-quality fruit in China, a national geographical indication product, known for its large fruit, bright color, thick flesh, juicy and sweet, known as "the first branch of northern spring fruit", "treasure in fruit", is one of the most representative specialties of Yantai.
